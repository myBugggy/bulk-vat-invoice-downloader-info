## 3. Detailed Description

```text
Stop clicking through dozens of reservations one by one every month or quarter! Bulk VAT Invoice Downloader for Airbnb Hosts automates the entire process of finding, generating, and downloading your Airbnb Host VAT invoices for accounting and tax compliance.

With a single click on your Airbnb Reservations page, the extension scans visible reservations, locates the official VAT invoice for each booking, converts every invoice into a clean, print-ready PDF named with its Invoice ID and Reservation Confirmation Code, and bundles everything into a single organized ZIP archive.

✨ KEY FEATURES
• One-Click Multi-Page Bulk Download: Automatically inspects all reservations on your Completed Reservations page and clicks the "Next" (Próximo) pagination button automatically.
• Configurable Pagination Limiter: Choose how many times the extension clicks the "Next" page button (default is 2 clicks = up to 3 pages per run) so you stay in full control.
• True PDF Generation: Converts each Airbnb VAT invoice page into a standard PDF file ready for your bookkeeper, accountant, or tax authority.
• Smart File Naming: Automatically names each PDF with both the Invoice ID and Reservation Code (e.g., Airbnb_VAT_Invoice_INV12345_Res_HMABCDEFGH.pdf) so you can match payouts effortlessly.
• Single Bundled ZIP Archive: No browser download spam—all PDFs from a run are packaged into a single timestamped ZIP file (e.g., Airbnb_Invoices_2026-10-31.zip).
• Custom Download Subfolder: Optionally configure a dedicated subfolder inside your Downloads directory via the Extension Options page.
• Multi-Country Domain Support: Works seamlessly across 20 international Airbnb domains (.com, .pt, .es, .fr, .it, .co.uk, .de, .nl, .ch, .at, .be, .dk, .se, .no, .fi, .ca, .com.au, .com.br, .co.in, .mx) and supports localized reservation & pagination buttons.
• Real-Time Progress & Activity Log: Track every page, reservation inspected, and invoice downloaded directly inside the popup window.

🚀 HOW TO USE (IMPORTANT)
1. Install the extension and start your 3-day free trial (or enter your License Key in the popup).
2. Click **"Go to Completed Reservations ↗"** inside the extension popup (or navigate to **airbnb.com/hosting/reservations/completed**).
3. **Do not forget to use the Airbnb Filters button to choose your Start & End Dates first!**
4. Verify your **Max "Next" page clicks** limit (default is 2) and press **"Download Invoices"**.
5. Keep the page open while the extension extracts each VAT invoice, automatically advances through pages up to your limit, and saves your bundled ZIP file!

💼 PRICING & SUBSCRIPTION

• Continued access requires an active subscription (€3.99/month, cancel anytime), managed securely via Lemon Squeezy.

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY
• 100% Local Processing: Your Airbnb session, guest details, reservation codes, and VAT invoice PDFs are processed entirely inside your own browser and saved directly to your computer.
• Zero Reservation Data Collection: We never transmit, store, or view your Airbnb reservations or financial documents on any external server.
```
